About Me

Building platforms that work at scale.

I'm a Senior Web Developer and IT Consultant based in Northern Virginia (Alexandria, VA), with over 14 years of experience designing and delivering complex web applications for large organizations.

I specialize in PHP/Symfony back-end architecture, Drupal CMS development, and building clean, maintainable systems that solve real organizational problems. I work with equal comfort on the backend and the frontend, and I bring strong UI/UX instincts to everything I build.
